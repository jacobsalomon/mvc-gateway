# MVC SMS Messaging and Opt-In Terms

The Mechanical Vision Corporation, also called "MVC," offers a low-volume text messaging service for people who expressly request it. Messages are conversational and transactional. They may include assistant replies, requested briefings, reminders, account notifications, and support responses. MVC does not use this program for unsolicited marketing.

## How to Opt In

Text **START** to **+1 (877) 315-2826** from a mobile number you own or are authorized to use. By doing so, you consent to receive automated and manually initiated text messages from MVC at that number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

## Your Choices

- Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages unless you opt in again.
- Reply **START** to opt in again after opting out.
- Reply **HELP** for help or contact jake@mechanicalvisioncorp.com.

## Privacy

MVC does not sell SMS opt-in data or share SMS consent or phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use this information only to operate, support, secure, and comply with law for the messaging service, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Service Limits

Delivery is subject to carrier and network availability. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. MVC may suspend the service to prevent abuse, protect security, or comply with law.

This messaging service is not an emergency service. Do not use it for urgent medical, safety, or emergency needs. Health and fitness coaching messages are general informational support and do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Call 911 or your local emergency number when immediate help is needed.

Use of the messaging service is also subject to MVC's [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
